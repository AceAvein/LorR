Revisions This document presents the revised environmental settings, training mechanics, controller interactions, NPC dialogue scripts, dominant hand baseline testing, phase progression, and scene transition logic for the norR-VR Training System. The virtual companion throughout the application is Dr. Alex Rivera, a VR Motor Skills Training Coach. Clinical Session Structure & System Lock Logic 1. Initial Training Session (Week 1 a Orientation & Baseline): ● The user performs Dominant Hand Baseline Calibration for applicable training drills to establish reference benchmarks. ● Phase I (Baseline Orientation) is introduced across all four training modules. ● Phase I requires a minimum of four training sessions. ● Future phases (Phases II, III, and IV) are unlocked progressively once the user satisfies the required performance criteria. ● The user must not automatically advance to the next phase simply because another day has passed. 2. Real-Time Guided Tutorial Remediation: ● During guided tutorial steps, if the user performs an incorrect action, such as using the wrong controller, placing an object in the wrong box, or selecting an incorrect target, Dr. Rivera provides encouraging real-time audio guidance. ● The NPC explains what went wrong and how to correct the action. ● During unguided training, the NPC only observes the user's performance and does not provide continuous corrective dialogue. 3. Phase-to-Phase Training Transitions (Success vs. Immediate Retry Branches): Advancement Met (Success Branch): ● Upon completing a phase session and meeting all required advancement metrics, Dr. Rivera confirms the user's progress. ● The next training phase becomes available. ● The system displays the next available training activity. NPC Dialogue: Dr. Alex Rivera: “Vreat work! You have successfully completed this training phase and met the required performance criteria. Your results have been recorded, and the next phase is now available. You may continue to the next phase or return to the main menu.” ||PAGE|| Advancement Unmet (Immediate Practice Branch): ● If the user fails to satisfy any required metric, Dr. Rivera provides encouraging feedback with actionable tips. ● The user is prompted to retry the current phase or return to the main menu. ● The next phase remains locked until the required criteria are satisfied. NPC Dialogue: Dr. Alex Rivera: “Vood effort! You have completed the training session, but some of the required performance criteria have not yet been met. Review your results and feedback, then retry the current phase when you are ready.” 4. Unguided Progressive Training Sessions (Week 1 to Week 4+): ● Training progression depends on performance rather than calendar dates. ● The system may recommend a training schedule, such as returning after seven days. ● Recommended training dates must not automatically lock the user's next phase. ● Demo Mode may allow manually adjusted or simulated training dates for capstone presentation purposes. NPC Dialogue a Recommended Next Session: Dr. Alex Rivera: “Your next training session is recommended for [Date]. Remember, your training progression depends on your performance and completion of the required criteria, not simply the passing of time.” 2. General Rules ● The training program has 4 phases. ● Each phase is planned to take 1 week. ● Each phase has 4 training sessions. ● 1 session = 1 training day. ● Sessions must be scheduled on separate days. ● Rest days are provided between training days. ● Fsers must complete the required sessions before moving to the next phase. ● Fsers do not automatically advance Just because a week has passed. ● If requirements are not met, the user repeats the phase for another week. ● There are options to add more sessions per phase. Phase I a Baseline and Orientation ● 4 sessions per week. ● Introducing the basic exercises. ● Record baseline performance. ● Eomplete all Phase I activities consistently. ● Document the required baseline measurements. ● Progress when Phase I requirements are met. Phase II a Foundational Skill Development ● 4 sessions per week. ● Follow the prescribed Phase II exercise settings. ● Record performance after every session. ● Evaluate the specified performance improvements. ● Progress when the phase requirements are met. ||PAGE|| Phase III a Intermediate Performance ● 4 sessions per week. ● Follow the prescribed Phase III exercise settings. ● Record performance after every session. ● Required performance criteria must be achieved in 2 consecutive sessions. ● The 2 successful sessions must take place on different training days. ● Progress only after both sessions meet the requirements. Phase IV a Advanced Integration ● 4 sessions per week. ● Follow the prescribed Phase IV exercise settings. ● Record performance after every session. ● Final assessment requirements must be achieved in 2 consecutive assessment sessions. ● The 2 successful sessions must take place on different training days. ● Program completion requires meeting the final requirements. ● If requirements are not met, repeat Phase IV for another week. Phase Progression Rules ● Eheck phase requirements after every completed session. ● Do not increase difficulty automatically after each session. ● Keep the user on the same phase until its requirements are met. ● For Phase III and IV, check the results of the 2 most recent consecutive sessions. ● Both sessions must satisfy the required criteria. ● If one session fails the criteria, the consecutive successful-session count resets. ● Once requirements are met, unlock the next phase. ● If requirements are not met within the planned week, repeat the phase for another week. 4.
