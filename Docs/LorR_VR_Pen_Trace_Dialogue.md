# Pen Control Path Tracing — Dr. Alex Rivera Dialogue

**Source:** `LorR VR Storylines & Dialogue Blueprint` (uploaded PDF)  
**NPC:** Dr. Alex Rivera — VR Motor Skills Training Coach  
**Drill:** Simulation 1 — Pen Control Path Tracing (Precision Layer)

> Training rules / metrics / unlocks still follow the Comprehensive Project Reference (bible).  
> This file is spoken dialogue + module framing from the Dialogue Blueprint.

---

## Environment

- Training room
- Desk or table, chair
- Virtual tracing tablet or paper
- Virtual pen
- NPC positioned in front of the user
- Should resemble a professional training facility

---

## NPC Dialogue — Environment Introduction

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“Welcome to the Pen Tracing station. Please get comfortable at your desk. In this activity, you will use the virtual pen to follow the tracing path with your non-dominant hand. Focus on maintaining controlled movements and staying within the path.”

---

## NPC Dialogue — Controller Instruction

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“Use your non-dominant controller for this activity. Press and hold the Trigger using your index finger to begin tracing. Release the Trigger when you want to stop drawing. Follow the path carefully and try to maintain consistent movement.”

---

## NPC Dialogue — Incorrect Controller

*(only if the detected controller is the dominant hand)*

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“Please switch to your non-dominant controller before starting the tracing task.”

---

## NPC Dialogue — Tracing Error

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“You moved outside the tracing path. Slow down and guide the pen back toward the line. Focus on controlled movement rather than rushing.”

---

## NPC Dialogue — Phase Completion

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“Excellent work! You have completed the tracing activity. Your path accuracy, deviation, and completion time have been recorded. Review your performance before continuing.”

---

## Shared session checkpoint lines (all drills)

### Advancement Met (Success)

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“Great work! You have successfully completed this training phase and met the required performance criteria. Your results have been recorded, and the next phase is now available. You may continue to the next phase or return to the main menu.”

### Advancement Unmet (Retry)

**Dr. Alex Rivera:**  
“Good effort! You have completed the training session, but some of the required performance criteria have not yet been met. Review your results and feedback, then retry the current phase when you are ready.”
